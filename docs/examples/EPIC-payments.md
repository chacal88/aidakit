# Payments system

Goal: let customers pay for their appointment online, so the barbershop stops chasing cash.

Why now: the top churn reason in support tickets is "no-show, no way to pre-charge". Priority: high.

## Features

- **Feature:** Checkout — changes: feature-cart, feature-checkout-ui
- **Feature:** Refunds — changes: feature-refund
- **Feature:** Subscriptions — changes: feature-subscription-plan

## Notes

change shows as **backlog** on the roadmap until `aidakit:plan` creates its artifacts.
